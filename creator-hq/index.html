Activity-led shoot plan

First filming setup

  • Phone vertical, rear camera if easy; lock focus/exposure.
  • Frame chest-up while hands are actively arranging/annotating objects.
  • Face a window; avoid overhead mixed lighting.
  • Record clean speech in a quiet room. Add page flips, button clicks and taps during edit, not live.
  • Capture clean inserts: hands, cards, notes, detail shots, pauses.
  • Record narration in short story beats, not one perfect uninterrupted take.
