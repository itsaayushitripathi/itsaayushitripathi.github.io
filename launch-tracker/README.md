# Launch OS: Startup Launch Tracker

**Live dashboard:** https://itsaayushitripathi.github.io/launch-tracker/

A general-purpose launch planning dashboard for founders and lean teams who need a starting point for turning a business idea into an organized launch.

## What it includes
- A cross-functional sample launch plan spanning 11 departments.
- Four operating buckets: **Build, Sell, Deliver, Control**.
- Dashboard roll-up of task volume, completion, in-progress work, and open high-priority tasks.
- Department-level completion pulse and per-bucket progress.
- Filter and search by department, work bucket, status, and priority.
- Add or edit tasks with department, bucket, phase, priority, owner, due date, and status.
- Browser-local saving, so edits persist in the same browser on the same device.
- CSV export for spreadsheet use.
- Reset to restore the generic sample plan.

## Work bucket definitions
- **Build:** Establish the brand, offer, product/service, platform, and foundations.
- **Sell:** Create demand, communicate value, acquire customers, and convert.
- **Deliver:** Fulfil the promise through operations, shipping/service delivery, and customer experience.
- **Control:** Govern risk, compliance, cash, quality, measurement, and business decisions.

## Departments
Strategy & Brand; Product & Development; Finance & Legal; E-commerce & Technology; Marketing & Growth; Content & Creative; Supply Chain & Fulfilment; Customer Experience; Data & Analytics; People & Administration.

## Getting started
1. Open the live dashboard.
2. Search and filter the sample plan to orient yourself.
3. Edit tasks to suit your company, add owners and due dates, and update statuses.
4. Export a CSV backup or working copy for your spreadsheet.
5. Use the reset button only when you want to overwrite the current browser-saved workspace with the sample.

## Important notes
- Tasks are generalized startup launch examples, not a prescribed checklist. Applicability depends on your industry, geography, business model, regulatory context, and launch scope.
- The dashboard saves data in browser local storage. It does not provide shared team sync, account access, or a server-side database. Export CSV periodically if the data matters.
- Reset replaces the locally saved task data. Export first if you want to keep it.
- This is a planning aid, not legal, tax, finance, product-safety, or regulatory advice.

## Portfolio context
The framework was shaped by cross-functional launch planning work at a D2C brand, then generalized and sanitized for use by other startups. Internal task wording, names, company-specific owners, and confidential information are not included.
