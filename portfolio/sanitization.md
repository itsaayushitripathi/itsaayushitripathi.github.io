# Sanitization and Public Portfolio Boundaries

Portfolio content is designed to show the structure, reasoning, and systems behind the work without disclosing confidential or personal information.

## Excluded or generalized
- Team identities replaced by role-based ownership or removed.
- Partner, vendor, formulator and creator identities anonymized or excluded.
- Cost inputs and team costs removed or replaced with clearly illustrative values.
- Internal targets and budgets replaced with placeholders.
- Private working notes, document references and local filesystem paths cleared.
- Formulation and supplier ingredient details excluded.
- Contracts, legal reviews, consumer test data, participant images and feedback records excluded.
- Credentials, API keys and recovery codes excluded.
- Embedded author metadata and cached source formula values removed where applicable.

## Retained only where public or generalized
- General brand and launch frameworks, sanitized task wording, dashboard architecture, non-confidential brand elements and generalized work descriptions.
- Sample data is labelled or treated as illustrative.
- The startup launch tracker is generalized for broad startup use rather than exposing the original company's internal tracker.

## Public portfolio interpretation
- The portfolio demonstrates work artifacts and system design. It does not claim specific business outcomes unless a verifiable, approved metric is explicitly stated.
- Reusable startup checklist tasks are examples. Legal, regulatory, finance, product-safety and operational requirements vary by industry and geography and should be verified by qualified professionals.
- Internal work files and confidential materials are not intended for public sharing.
