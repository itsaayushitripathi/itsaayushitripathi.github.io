# Work Inventory — D2C Launch Systems Portfolio

**Work period represented:** September–November 2026, pre-launch.  
**Portfolio convention:** “Template” means a sanitized template or artifact in the source portfolio ZIP; “Covered” means the structure is represented by a template/showcase; “Private” means excluded; “Archive” means superseded. This overview does not claim every source file is independently published as a downloadable file.

## 1. Dashboards, trackers & financial models

### Master launch task manager
- Original workbook: 390-task launch tracker across 9 departments.
- Dashboard, tracker overview, distribution table, separate Build / Sell / Deliver / Control work buckets and lists.
- Portfolio version is generalized for other startups: 61 broad starter tasks, 11 departments, interactive browser-based dashboard, search/filter, edit/add, status, owner, due date, local save and CSV export.
- Original source wording and company-specific task/owner details are not reproduced in the generalized live tool.

### Marketing and e-commerce dashboard workbook
Two independent dashboards supported by 10 connector/raw-data tabs:
- **Marketing Dashboard:** revenue, spend, ROAS, conversion, overall funnel, Meta/Google/YouTube paid analytics, independent four-week date window.
- **E-commerce Dashboard:** orders, AOV, repeat purchase rate, cart abandonment, payment success, RTO, blended site-wide funnel, payment method health, SKU performance, website signals and revenue trend.
- Raw/source areas cover Shopify orders, Meta ads, Google Ads, GA4 sessions and website detail, Razorpay payments, YouTube public stats/analytics, Instagram and Facebook insights.
- Google Ads to Sheets sync script.

### D2C P&L model (14 tabs)
- Monthly D2C channel P&L from units sold and traffic through net revenue, COGS, gross profit, variable channel costs, CM1, marketing/CAC, CM2 and EBITDA.
- SKU-level unit economics and SKU x channel performance.
- Key metrics dashboard with benchmark reference ranges and monthly scorecard.
- Marketing spend detail, returns/RTO detail, cohort LTV by source, creator UTM rollup, qualitative notes, search/content lift tracker.
- Benchmarking contains context/caveats; all model inputs used in portfolio examples are illustrative.

### Waitlist program dashboard
- Signups, signup funnel, acquisition channels, Founding Member count/numbering and email performance.
- Dashboard data is entirely sample data.
- Waitlist program template includes Shopify setup, consent design, traffic planning, nurture sequencing and the AI Style Profile concept.
- The biometric Face Scan prototype itself is private and intentionally excluded.

### Keyword & topic intelligence dashboard
- Interactive keyword map and local topic explorer sourced from Ubersuggest/Semrush connectors.
- Search demand snapshot and topic grouping to support SEO/SEM/AEO/GEO strategy.
- Blog strategy: six clusters, pre-launch sequencing, citation assets, indexing checklist and guardrails.
- Keyword volumes are a single-source snapshot, not real-time market data.

## 2. Gamified Shopify experience

### Drive Before You Buy
- **Type:** Side project / engagement experiment; built August–September 2026.
- **Platform:** Shopify Balance theme, custom layout/password.liquid.
- Replaced the conventional locked storefront/password landing with a small driving game to invite dwell time and launch email signup.
- Visitor journey: driver's-eye highway hero and launching billboard; Sunday Drive Radio player; Cars/Bikes tabs with rotatable 3D model cards; Tap to drive; selected vehicle footage with accelerator and brake; Shopify native newsletter signup; standard team password dialog.
- Vehicle lineup documented in source: Porsche 911, Ferrari 458 Italia, Lamborghini Urus, Morris Minor, Ford Mustang; Royal Enfield, Ducati and Harley-Davidson.
- Accelerator: requestAnimationFrame loop ramping playback speed from 1× to 5× over 2.2 seconds; coast-down on release; immediate brake; selected vehicle exhaust sound loops separately from muted video; rain/traffic overlays.
- 3D via Google model-viewer and GLB assets; hidden YouTube IFrame player controlled via postMessage.
- Performance improvements: lazy-load 3D viewer, incremental model loading/unloading, deferred YouTube player, responsive WebP hero, overlay pedals, early CDN preconnect.
- Public case study includes a recreated interactive concept preview and a description of the original implementation. Original store media/theme source is not published.
