# Work Inventory — Nodd Men Launch Systems

**Work period represented:** September–November 2026, pre-launch.  
This catalogue summarizes the portfolio source inventory. “Template / framework” means sanitized or generalized portfolio material; the catalogue itself is not a claim that every underlying original artifact is published as a downloadable file.

## Strategy and brand
- Brand bible architecture: brand/customer character, beliefs, product system, voice, channel rules, visual identity, strategic foundations.
- Design tokens and design system.
- Brand narrative, brand story, origin / About-page structure.
- Brand architecture, core brand idea, philosophy and human-behaviour framework.
- Creative direction and visual playbooks for AI-assisted imagery.
- Department and system maps.
- Community genuineness evaluation framework and case studies.

## Research and planning
- ICP / audience analysis structure and customer archetype mapping.
- Sunscreen market, share-of-voice and keyword planning.
- SEO, SEM, GEO and AEO analysis frameworks.
- Blog-topic strategy with clusters, pre-launch sequencing, citation assets and indexing checklist.
- Keyword dashboard and interactive topic explorer.
- Competitive analysis and product gap review structures.
- Analytics architecture and data-analysis blueprints.

## Dashboards and trackers
- Cross-functional launch task manager organized across departments and Build / Sell / Deliver / Control buckets.
- Marketing and e-commerce dashboards with source-data architecture.
- Google Ads to Sheets reporting script.
- D2C P&L model structure: channel P&L, unit economics, contribution margin, CAC, LTV:CAC, cohort LTV and creator ROI.
- Launch desk, board and content-tracker structure.
- Waitlist program dashboard.

## Launch, content and copy
- Launch plan with goals, go/no-go gates, phases, channels, risks and decisions.
- Three-chapter launch narrative and meeting/content decision structures.
- Main film, cut-down, hook, reel, vox-pop and manufacturing-story script frameworks.
- Waitlist program framework including Shopify setup, consent, traffic planning and nurture.
- Website copy plan for homepage and PDP modules, SKU maps, CTA library and hook improvements.
- Website layout specifications, wireframes and storefront prototype structures.
- AI product imagery briefs and production guidelines.

## Operations, legal and product
- AI-assisted daily store-health check workflow, drafted for human review.
- Warehouse setup, shipping/inventory stack and fulfilment playbook structures.
- Warehouse compliance checklist framework.
- Store policy structures.
- Product formulation, raw material, consumer study, legal and contract work are intentionally excluded from public artifacts where sensitive.

## Skills evidenced
E-commerce operations; analytics and data modelling; financial modelling; cross-functional project management; SEO/AEO/GEO and content strategy; brand and conversion copy; growth and lifecycle; AI workflows and automation.

Shopify, Shopify Flow, Google Sheets, Excel, Google Apps Script, Google Ads Scripts, GA4, Meta Ads, Razorpay, inventory/fulfilment systems, lifecycle messaging, SEO research connectors and Claude with MCP connectors.
