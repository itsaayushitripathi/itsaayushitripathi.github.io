The visitor journey

From curiosity to signup.

  • Drive hero: driver's-eye highway imagery with launching-soon billboard and Notify Me CTA.
  • Sunday Drive Radio: play/pause, ±10 second seek, progress and volume through hidden YouTube IFrame player.
  • Pick your engine: Cars/Bikes tabs with rotatable 3D vehicle models and Tap to drive.
  • Drive mode: selected vehicle footage, hold accelerator to ramp 1× to 5×, coast on release, brake to stop; rain/traffic overlays.
  • Launch signup: Shopify native customer form, newsletter tag and inline success/error states.
Implementation

Interaction, not just decoration.

  • Custom Shopify layout/password.liquid shell, preserving theme password functionality.
  • Google model-viewer and GLB 3D assets.
  • requestAnimationFrame throttle controller, accelerating over 2.2s, coast-down 0.9–2.6s and instant brake.
  • Muted vehicle video with separate matching exhaust audio loop.
  • Hidden YouTube iframe controlled via postMessage commands and load retries.
